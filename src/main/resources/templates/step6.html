Step 6: Ask the user

A fake deployment runs a few steps, then stops and asks for approval. The question arrives over SSE; your answer goes back as a normal POST. The job thread waits in between, and continues once the answer is in.

What to notice

  • SSE is one-way. The "two-way" part is a second channel: the answer is an ordinary POST /step6/jobs/{id}/answer.
  • The job thread blocks in PendingQuestion.await (a CompletableFuture). The POST handler completes that future, which wakes the job up.
  • Try all three outcomes: approve, reject, and doing nothing for 60 s. After a timeout the question is expired, so a late click gets "too late" instead of being silently ignored.
